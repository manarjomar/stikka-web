Your photos, turned into stickers in seconds

Stikka cuts out the person, pet or object in any photo by itself. You fine-tune it, add text and emojis, and keep it in packs for your chats. No ads, no accounts, and your photos never leave your phone.

What you can do

✂️ Smart cutout

Pick a photo, take one with the camera or share it to Stikka. The cutout is automatic and happens on your phone.

🖌️ Precise editing

Brush to erase or restore, lasso, rectangle or square, pinch to zoom and a magnifier.

🎨 Decoration

Text with colours, fonts and speech bubbles, emojis, outline and shadow. All editable later.

🎬 Animated

Animated stickers from a video or a GIF, with or without background.

📦 Packs

Group your stickers with a name, author and icon, and add them to WhatsApp in one tap.

💾 Backup and sharing

Import the stickers from your chats, share packs with anyone who has Stikka and back up everything.

Your privacy

Stikka has no accounts, shows no ads, has no servers of its own and doesn't use the internet. Your photos, cutouts and packs stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
