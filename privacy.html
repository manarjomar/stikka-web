Stikka privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: Stikka has no accounts, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics of its own, and doesn't use the internet. It doesn't collect or share your photos or stickers. Everything you create stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

The Stikka app (app.stikka) is developed by Manuel Arjona. For any privacy question, write to arjona.apps@gmail.com.

What data the app uses and where it is stored

Stikka works without sign-up. The photos you pick, cutouts, decorations, stickers, packs and settings are stored only in the app's internal storage on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else. The app has no internet permission and Android cloud auto-backup is turned off, so they don't leave that way either.

Photos, videos and camera

Stikka only accesses the photos, videos or GIFs you choose with the system picker, the photo you take with the camera app, or the image you share with it. It can't see the rest of your gallery. The app doesn't request storage or camera permissions.

Smart cutout (Google ML Kit)

The automatic cutout is done by Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone: the image is processed on the device and is not sent to any server. The cutout model is downloaded and installed by Google Play services. According to Google, ML Kit may collect technical information about the device and the app, and performance metrics, for diagnostics and usage statistics; this data is encrypted and Google doesn't share it with third parties. It doesn't include your photos. More information on the ML Kit data page and in Google's privacy policy.

WhatsApp stickers

If you import WhatsApp stickers, Stikka only reads the sticker folder you choose with the system folder picker, and copies the ones you select into the app. When you add a pack to WhatsApp, Stikka hands the stickers of that pack, with its name, author and icon, to WhatsApp on your phone. From then on, WhatsApp's privacy policy applies. Stikka is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Backups and shared packs

Backups and packs you export are files saved or shared wherever you decide (for example, your Drive or a message). Stikka doesn't send them on its own.

Error reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves a technical report on the phone (app and Android version, phone model and the error). It is only sent if you tap “Send to the developer”, using your email app. The same applies to contact emails from the app, which add the app version and phone model to help solve the problem.

Children

Stikka is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Since the developer doesn't hold your data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on any server. To remove everything Stikka stores, clear its data from Android settings or uninstall the app. If you write by email, that email is used only to reply to you and you can ask for it to be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with its new date.
